## Judgement calls

- **Elves ▸ Jungle** — Notion lists Traversal twice in this archetype; the duplicate is dropped.
- **Goblin ▸ Hobgoblin** — Notion offers a final choice of Finesse Training, Martial Training or Skilled, all at 2. Martial Training is carried as the default.
- **Undine ▸ Elemental** — Notion re-lists Amphibious (2) and Swim Speed (2) here; both duplicates are dropped, which is exactly what brings this to 16.
- **Undine ▸ Iceborn** — Notion re-lists Amphibious (2) here; the duplicate is dropped.
- **Undine ▸ Poisonborn** — Priced under item 7. The source text also lists Darkvision and a separate Poison Resilience; poison resistance and the condition advantage are folded into the attunement, and Darkvision is dropped, because the budget buys the genasi spell package or Darkvision but not both. Undine ▸ Elemental takes no Darkvision either.
- **Ifriti ▸ Magma** — Volcanic Being is confirmed at 0, not merely unpriced: you already have fire resistance from the shared attunement, so this is a narrow flavour rider like Combat Awareness (0) or Acclimatization (0), and 0 is what lands the chain on exactly 16.
- **Ifriti ▸ Steam** — Heat Distortion is confirmed at 0 on the same reasoning as Volcanic Being.
- **Talos ▸ Ironborn** — Metal Skin priced at 7 under item 7: it is Ironborn’s single signature trait, standing where every other Talos archetype has a 7-point Elemental Magic, and resistance to all damage plus level-scaled healing is comparable to Durable (7).
- **Talos ▸ Goldenblood** — Goldenblood’s Metal Skin is a different, weaker trait than Ironborn’s — temporary HP rather than healing — so it is priced at 4 while Ironborn’s is 7.
- **Talos ▸ Duster / Saltborn** — Priced under item 7. Necrotic Resistance is taken at the curated 3 (the value the Tiefling and Fetchling pages use), which lands the chain on exactly 16.
- **Talos ▸ Metal** — Priced under item 7. Metallic Skin at 3 matches Natural Armor (3), the other AC-raising trait in the vocabulary.
- **Talos ▸ Oozeborn** — Priced under item 7. The source also lists Darkvision; it is dropped so Slippery Skin — the archetype’s signature — fits inside 16.
- **Asir ▸ Psionic** — Notion gives two different archetypes the same name, "Psionic". Kept verbatim per the Notion-naming rule, with the id disambiguated. Worth renaming one in Notion — the builder previously called this one "Empath".
- **Asir ▸ Windborn** — Priced under item 7. Notion gives only spell lists; Limited Flight (5) is added as the archetype’s signature — Windborn is the air genasi and flight is what the remaining 5 points buy.
- **Returned ▸ Plasmoid form** — Notion re-lists Constructed Form inside this archetype; the duplicate is dropped and the archetype’s wording carried as an override.
- **Mrrshan ▸ Felis** — The builder called this archetype "Tabaxi". Notion’s name is Felis.
- **Ysoki ▸ Ysoki ▸ Spotter** — Notion’s "Hunter’s Senses (7)" is the curated Keen Smell (3) plus Hunter’s Senses (4).
- **Iruxi ▸ Badlands** — Notion lists both Acclimatization (0) and Survivalist (1). They are two options of one Cultural Skill trait, but Notion charges for both, so both are kept — de-duplication only drops an exact id+option repeat.
- **Iruxi ▸ Forest or Plains** — The builder previously shortened this to "Forest"; Notion’s name is "Forest or Plains".
- **Muul ▸ Auran ▸ Wildhorn** — Notion prices "Skill Proficiency (4)" for two skills; carried as two 2-point curated proficiencies.
- **Muul ▸ Loxodon ▸ Loxodon** — Windwise and Soulwise exist only in the brew file, not in Notion. Dropped.
- **Ysoki ▸ Harengon ▸ Harengon** — The builder had Hare and Rabbit archetypes. Notion defines none; they are dropped.
- **Ysoki ▸ Kercpa ▸ Kercpa** — The builder spelled this "Kcerpa". Notion spells it Kercpa.
- **Anadi ▸ Anadi** — Notion splits these under "In your spider form" and "In your humanoid form", but both are granted together — they are not alternatives — so they are one flat trait list, not two archetypes.
